Organization-picker decision
The existing GET /api/v1/platform/organization_units endpoint cannot safely
serve the future company-scoped HR picker. It is tenant-wide, has only id/type
filters, and requires platform.organization_unit.read. Although it returns the
needed hierarchy fields, it does not constrain results to a selected company
and descendants. A future separately approved 02F-B slice needs the minimum
read-only projection over the existing organization_unit authority: required
company_id, optional type/status/search, selected company plus descendants,
stable hierarchy fields, an HR read permission, contextual company
authorization, and existing RLS. No new table or mutation is justified.
