FINAL REVIEW
- Security: protected-by-default routes, contextual company authorization, transaction-local forced RLS, distinct approval actors and safe API failures are verified. Frontend permission visibility is convenience only; server checks remain authoritative.
- Integrity: composite tenant keys, company/date scope triggers, exclusion constraints, exact numeric leave days, immutable source evidence, transactional period lock/snapshot generation and deterministic content hashing are enforced.
- Scalability: company/date indexes and bounded pagination are used; period derivation is intentionally transactional and may later move to an idempotent background worker for very large companies without changing the snapshot contract.
