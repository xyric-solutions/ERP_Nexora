VERIFIED — authoritative end-to-end flow
----------------------------------------
Company legal profile is read first. The effective employee assignment supplies
the explicit work-location identity. The effective reviewed work-location
profile supplies country/jurisdiction evidence. Only approved, assignment-
scoped overrides may alter a rule scope. The resolver selects the exact active
company activation, recursively resolves approved released pack dependencies,
and selects effective deterministic rule versions. The statutory provider
passes this normalized evidence to the country-neutral executor. Payroll adds
normalized employee/employer lines and seals exact context, pack, rule, input,
output and result evidence. Protected APIs and the UI read that authoritative
evidence; they do not independently resolve law or mutate sealed snapshots.

No duplicate hidden authority was found. app/hr/payroll.py contains no country
or jurisdiction branch. Currency reaches ordinary Payroll validation but is not
read by the localization resolver/provider as a jurisdiction detector. Address
fields exist only as explicit profile data; the resolution path does not read
address, locality, postal code, locale, language, time zone, IP, browser, device
or AI output.
