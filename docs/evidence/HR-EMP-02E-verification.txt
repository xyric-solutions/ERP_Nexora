HR-EMP-02E - Employment Context and Consumer Boundary
Verification date: 2026-09-30
Status: CLOSED WITH WAIVERS

IMPLEMENTED
- Deterministic read-only EmploymentContextProvider.resolve(tenant_id, company_id,
  employee_id, as_of) with mandatory day-granular as_of.
- One tenant-scoped PostgreSQL transaction resolves Employee, employment
  relationship/legal employer, lifecycle, authoritative relationship-bound
  assignment, organization placement, job/designation/grade, employment type,
  work location, the existing HR-LOC jurisdiction resolver, and money-free
  salary-authority/readiness evidence.
- Exact-cardinality resolution fails closed for zero/multiple relationships,
  assignments and other required authorities. Legacy compatibility assignments
  are excluded. Lifecycle chains are independently reconstructed and checked.
- Payroll eligibility is always not_assessed. No salary, gross/net pay, tax,
  deduction, statutory-contribution, payslip or accounting amount is returned.
- Narrow GET /api/v1/hr/employment_contexts API with required company_id,
  employee_id and YYYY-MM-DD as_of, dedicated RBAC, contextual company
  authorization, RLS-backed reads, private/no-store caching and stable ETag.
- No existing Payroll or Attendance consumer was migrated.
- No schema change or migration was required.
