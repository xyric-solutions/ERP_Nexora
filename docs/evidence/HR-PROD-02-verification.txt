Authorities and calculation rule
--------------------------------
- Single salary authority: existing `hr_salary_assignment` rows.
- Employment boundary: Slice 1 period-aware legal-employment candidates and
  EmploymentContextProvider evidence.
- Proration convention: existing component calculation rule
  `amount x paid_days / calendar_days`.
- Salary portions are inclusive intersections of payroll period, effective
  employment, and approved/effective/superseded salary assignments.
- Adjustments and loan instalments remain once-per-period authorities and are
  applied once after the salary portions are aggregated.
- No salary, employment, attendance, leave, localization, or calculation
  authority was duplicated.

Fail-closed rules
-----------------
- Missing salary: `SALARY_AUTHORITY_MISSING`.
- Overlap: `SALARY_AUTHORITY_OVERLAPPING`.
- Salary outside employment: `SALARY_AUTHORITY_OUTSIDE_EMPLOYMENT`.
- Invalid structure/component/currency: existing salary-invalid blocker.
- Non-proratable or attendance-dependent component across multiple rates:
  `SALARY_COMPONENT_PERIOD_AMBIGUOUS`.
- Multiple salary rates plus aggregate paid days below covered calendar days:
  `SALARY_PERIOD_INPUT_AMBIGUOUS`.
- No latest-row fallback or arbitrary overlap selection exists.
