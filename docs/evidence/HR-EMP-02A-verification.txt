HR-EMP-02A — Schema Preflight and Employment Relationship Foundation
Verification date: 2026-09-29 Asia/Karachi
Status: CLOSED WITH WAIVERS

Scope implemented
- Reused the existing HR-01 Employee, employee_assignment, hr_master_record,
  organization_unit and work-location authorities.
- Reused company_legal_profile and all HR-LOC jurisdiction resolution tables
  and services without modification.
- Confirmed `hr_salary_assignment` in HR production migration 0003 and the
  existing Payroll repository/service as the HR-03 compensation authority.
- Added `business_unit` and `division` to organization_unit without imposing a
  fixed company/branch/division/department hierarchy.
- Added the separate `hr_employment_relationship` legal-employment authority.
- Added no relationship API, lifecycle action, consumer cutover, employee UI,
  assignment redesign, compensation, Payroll, Attendance, Leave, country rule,
  custom field or cross-border capability.

Exact migrations
- Production Platform:
  `app/platform/postgres/migrations/0005_organization_unit_types.forward.sql`
  and `.rollback.sql`.
- Local/demo Platform:
  `app/platform/migrations/0018_organization_unit_types.forward.sql` and

- Production HR:
  `app/hr/postgres/migrations/0010_employment_relationship_foundation.forward.sql`

- No historical HR-LOC, HR, Platform, Attendance, Payroll or Finance migration
  was modified in place.

Database changes
- organization_unit type constraint remains `organization_unit_type_check` and
  now accepts company, business_unit, division, branch, department, warehouse.
- `organization_unit_acyclic` invokes
  `validate_organization_unit_acyclic()`. It takes a tenant-specific transaction
  advisory lock and rejects self/cyclic parentage. Migration preflight rejects
  a pre-existing cycle. Rollback refuses while business_unit/division rows exist.
- New table `hr_employment_relationship` contains tenant/id, employee,
  legal_employer, generated company type, relationship_number, controlled
  status, effective range, creator and timestamps.
- Named constraints:
  `hr_employment_relationship_pkey`,
  `uq_hr_employment_relationship_employer_id`,
  `uq_hr_employment_relationship_number`,
  `fk_hr_employment_relationship_tenant`,
  `fk_hr_employment_relationship_employee`,
  `fk_hr_employment_relationship_legal_employer`,
  `hr_employment_relationship_id_length`,
  `hr_employment_relationship_number_length`,
  `hr_employment_relationship_created_by_length`,
  `hr_employment_relationship_status_check`,
  `hr_employment_relationship_effective_range`,
  `hr_employment_relationship_ended_has_end`, and
  `ex_hr_employment_relationship_non_draft_period`.
- Named indexes:
  `idx_hr_employment_relationship_employee_effective` and
  `idx_hr_employment_relationship_employer_status_effective`, plus indexes from
  the primary, unique and GiST exclusion constraints.
- RLS is ENABLED and FORCED with policy
  `hr_employment_relationship_tenant_isolation` using the established
  transaction-local `nexora.tenant_id` context for USING and WITH CHECK.

Compatibility and consumer result
- No legacy relationship is guessed or backfilled. Existing Employee and
  assignment rows survive upgrade and the new relationship table stays empty.
- `employee.status`, `employee_assignment.company_id`, `branch_id`,
  `department_id`, all existing assignment keys/constraints and all existing
  consumer queries are unchanged.
- Attendance, Payroll, identity and HR-LOC keep using their current authorities
  in 02A. No hidden duplicate jurisdiction or compensation authority was added.
- The future `EmploymentContextProvider` remains mandatory but is not
  implemented prematurely in this schema-only slice.

Security review
- Relationship rows carry explicit typed legal-employer company scope and
  tenant-composite Employee/company references.
- Forced RLS denies reads and writes across tenants; legal-employer references
  reject non-company organization units.
- There is no new HTTP/service surface, so no new permission, maker-checker or
  audit action is introduced in 02A. Future mutation APIs must add company-
  scoped RBAC, idempotency and audit rather than exposing raw table writes.
- Relationship rows store no new personal identifier, secret or free-text legal
  evidence. Errors and logs were not changed.
