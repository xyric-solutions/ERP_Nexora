Resolution order and historical behavior
----------------------------------------
1. Select exactly one effective employment assignment for the employee/date.
2. Resolve its legal employer from the effective company legal profile.
3. Resolve the assignment's explicit work-location profile.
4. Apply only approved, effective employee overrides by statutory rule scope.
5. Require every selected jurisdiction to remain in the legal employer country;
   otherwise return cross_border_employment_model_required.
6. Require one exact active company activation for each target jurisdiction.
7. Build the valid national-to-leaf jurisdiction and pack-dependency stack.
8. Select approved/active, effective rule versions and reject invalid explicit
   replacement or ambiguous/conflicting evidence.
9. Split multi-date requests at assignment, profile, override, activation,
   dependency, pack and rule-version boundaries, preserving historical context.

The resolver never falls back to registered office, currency, locale, time
zone, address text, IP/device data, or AI. Missing or ambiguous evidence fails
closed. Identification, pack availability, activation state, and compliance
remain distinct; compliance is always reported as not_assessed by this slice.
