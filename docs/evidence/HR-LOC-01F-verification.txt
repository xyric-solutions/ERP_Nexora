Provider and Payroll behavior
-----------------------------
1. Determine whether localization is required for the payroll period from the
   explicit company control; never from currency, locale, address or tenant region.
2. Resolve the effective 01E legal-employer/assignment/work-location/override/
   activation/dependency/rule evidence for each employee.
3. Reject multiple effective segments until an approved allocation model exists.
4. Reject unresolved, cross-border, unapproved, expired, dependency-invalid,
   rule-missing, output-missing/unsupported, component-missing, or historical-evidence-
   missing contexts rather than selecting a fallback.
5. Evaluate only allow-listed deterministic rule shapes and merge normalized
   applicable/non-applicable lines with ordinary Payroll output.
6. Persist the payroll result and seal exact pack, rule, jurisdiction context,
   input hash, result hashes and normalized lines in the same transaction.

Historical proof
